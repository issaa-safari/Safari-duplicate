Three small labels used throughout the site: `StatusBadge` (departure/booking availability pill), `TrailBadge` (glassy pill over hero photography), `Eyebrow` (uppercase olive label above a heading).

```jsx
<StatusBadge status="low">3 seats left</StatusBadge>
<StatusBadge status="cancelled">Cancelled</StatusBadge>
<TrailBadge>Group Bike Tours</TrailBadge>
<Eyebrow>Upcoming Departures</Eyebrow>
```

`StatusBadge` colors: guaranteed/available = green, low = amber, full = grey, cancelled = red — always paired with the seat count in copy, not color alone.
